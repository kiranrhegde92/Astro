━━━ FREE TO USE ━━━

The daily brief, self chart, compatibility basics, and your first Akasha questions are free. Premium unlocks deeper readings, transit alerts, unlimited Akasha, and reduced ads.

━━━ WHO IT'S FOR ━━━

• Astrology-curious people who want more than sun signs
• Vedic readers who also want Western and Chinese context
• Journalers who want a reflective daily ritual
• Couples, friends, and matchmakers using compatibility
• Anyone tired of clickbait astrology

Not medical, legal, financial, or mental health advice. CosmicSelf is for personal insight and reflection.

Download CosmicSelf and turn astrology into a clean, personal daily practice.

## Google Play — Data Safety form answers

### Data collection summary
| Data type | Collected | Shared | Purpose | Optional? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Yes | No | App functionality, account management | No |
| Email address | Yes | No | Account management | No |
| User IDs | Yes | No | Account management, analytics | No |
| Birth date / time / place | Yes | No | App functionality (chart calculation) | No |
| Photos (if avatar) | Optional | No | Account management | Yes |
| App interactions | Yes | No | Analytics, app functionality | No |
| Crash logs | Yes | No | Analytics, quality | No |
| Notifications | Yes | No | Daily reminders, transit alerts | Yes (off by default) |

### Security practices
- **Data encrypted in transit:** Yes (HTTPS/TLS to Firebase and oracle endpoints)
- **Data encrypted at rest:** Yes (Firebase Firestore default encryption)
- **Users can request data deletion:** Yes (Settings → Delete account)
- **Committed to Play Families Policy:** N/A (not targeting children)
- **Independently validated against a global security standard:** No

### Ads
- Uses Google AdMob for non-personalized rewarded ads in the free tier.
